### 🌟 Featured Project: Agora (🏠 Marketplace Platform)

> **Agora** connects customers with vetted professional cleaning service providers with live job tracking, dynamic pricing, AI-verified trust gates, and automated payment escrow.

* **Turborepo & `pnpm` Monorepo Architecture**:
  * `app/` — **Flutter 3.38** cross-platform mobile client (iOS, Android, Web) built on a custom **Greek Design System (`GDS`)**
  * `search-web/` — **Next.js 16.4** Search Portal & instant dynamic pricing estimator
  * `marketing-site/` — **Next.js 16.4** landing site, SEO hub & customer/cleaner onboarding funnel
  * `admin/` — **Next.js 16.4** Operations, Trust & Safety Portal (7-gate cleaner readiness verification, split-screen dispute arbitration, live dispatch reassignment, DBS & property deed vetting, PECR/GDPR compliance consoles)
  * `api/` — **NestJS 12 + Fastify** GraphQL API gateway, custom `@GraphNode` ORM engine, and Layer-3 application orchestrators
  * `media/` — Automated **Remotion** 3-layer video compositing engine for marketing reels
  * `packages/` — Shared UI primitives & Aegean design tokens (`@agora/ui`, `@agora/tailwind-config`, `@agora/eslint-config`)
  * `terraform/` — Modular **GCP Infrastructure-as-Code** across staging and production environments
* **Graph Data Architecture**: Powered by **Neo4j** with a custom type-safe `@GraphNode` ORM & migration engine (`AgoraDbContext`), modeling complex user permissions, spatial service areas, multi-tier pricing, and relational review topologies.
* **AI, Multimodal OCR & MCP Automation**: **Gemini AI** document verification for UK Land Registry title deeds & IDs, AI dispute arbitration co-pilot, ReAct customer support concierge backed by a Neo4j `AgentMemory` subgraph, and a custom **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** operations server.
* **Fintech & Escrow**: End-to-end **Stripe Connect Express** onboarding, 24-hour pre-authorization escrow holds, automated platform fee splits, and atomic dispute settlement.
* **Geospatial Intelligence**: **Google Maps Platform** (Distance Matrix, Places, and Geocoding APIs) for travel-time routing, buffer calculation, and live ETA tracking.
* **GCP Cloud & DevOps**: Serverless deployment on **Cloud Run** (API & Server-Side GTM), **GKE** & **Skaffold**, **Global HTTPS Load Balancing & Cloud CDN**, **Cloud Tasks** (delayed settlement & lockout timers), **Cloud Scheduler** (cron pipelines), **Cloud Storage (GCS)**, **Secret Manager**, **Cloud KMS** (AES-256 lockbox vault), **Artifact Registry**, **Cloud DNS**, **Cloud Pub/Sub**, **Cloud Logging & Monitoring**, and on-demand **Compute Engine** Spot CI runners.
